# Anti-AI Prose Standards

This reference is the shared "anti-AI tone" criteria library for Writer and Editor. The Writer avoids these patterns during creation, and the Editor inspects against them in the aesthetic dimension, quoting source text as evidence.

## 1. Structural Cliches
- **Rule of Three / Parallel Triads**: Overusing three symmetric clauses for dramatic buildup ("He no longer hesitated, no longer retreated, no longer looked back"). Fix: Keep the single strongest clause; convert the rest into action or concrete detail.
- **Symmetric Block Stacking**: Paragraphs of identical length and grammatical shape that read like bullet points. Fix: Alternate short and long sentences so the prose breathes.
- **Numbered Subheadings inside Chapters**: Embedding numbered sections or markdown headers (`##`/`###`) inside the body prose. Fix: Rely solely on blank line breaks for scene shifts.

## 2. Diction & Word Choice Cliches
- **Stock Metaphors & Similes**: "Like a...", "as if...", "seemed to...". Fix: Use precise verbs or fresh sensory imagery.
- **Verbal Crutches & Filter Words**: "suddenly", "somehow", "felt a sense of", "could not help but", "a wave of". Fix: Cut the buffer words; let actions strike directly.
- **Abstract Editorializing**: "in a sense", "it is worth noting", "for some reason" — narrator summarizing for the reader. Fix: Remove editorial commentary; let concrete facts speak.
- **Binarized Formulas**: "What he wanted wasn't X, but Y", "This wasn't an end, but a beginning". Fix: Express meaning through concrete choices rather than formulaic rhetorical contrast.

## 3. Descriptive Cliches
- **Abstract Labels over Sensory Detail**: "The atmosphere was tense". Fix: Anchor in physical senses (tactile, olfactory, auditory).
- **Emotion Labeling**: "He felt angry/terrified". Fix: Depict involuntary somatic reactions and behavioral choices.

## 4. Dialogue Cliches
- **Voice Homogenization**: Stripping speaker tags leaves dialogue indistinguishable. Fix: Give each character distinct cadence, vocabulary, and subtext.
- **Over-explaining Motives**: Characters explicitly vocalizing their internal psychology. Fix: Bury motivation in actions and evasions.

## 5. Pacing & Emotional Modulation
- **Over-exposition**: Explaining every causal link exhaustively. Fix: Leave strategic narrative whitespace.
- **Forced Epiphanies**: Ending chapters with grand philosophical morals. Fix: Close on vivid imagery, an unresolved choice, or hanging tension.
