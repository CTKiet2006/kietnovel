# Narrative Consistency Mechanism

To maintain story coherence across prolonged serialized composition:

## Mandatory Pre-Writing Check

Before beginning each new chapter:
1. Review summaries of all completed chapters in the outline.
2. Read the preceding chapter to grasp active suspense threads.
3. Check character states (location, emotional status, relationships).

## Weaving Narrative Threads

In each new chapter:
- Call back to previously planted foreshadowing and clues.
- Mention past events organically within ongoing action.
- Ensure character behavior aligns with established traits and past decisions.

## Tracking Character States

Monitor character changes and evolution across chapters:
- Positional shifts (where characters are physically located).
- Emotional state (current mood, psychological fatigue).
- Relational shifts (changes in rapport, trust, or hostility).
- Capability shifts (new skills, critical knowledge, key assets).

## Sustaining the Suspense Chain

Advance overarching narrative enigmas step-by-step:
- Respond to or pay off at least one existing suspense thread per chapter.
- Introduce a new mystery or escalate existing stakes.
- Never abandon unsolved suspense threads without deliberate payoff.

## Consistency Checklist

- [ ] Character agency matches established personality profiles.
- [ ] Foreshadowing resolves with internal logical closure.
- [ ] Peaks and troughs balance rhythmically across scenes.
- [ ] Timeline progression remains chronologically coherent.
- [ ] Scene transitions flow organically without abrupt teleportation.
