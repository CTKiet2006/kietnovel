# Dialogue Crafting Standards

Compelling dialogue is a vital engine for characterizing figures, driving plots, and igniting friction.

## Core Dialogue Principles

### 1. Mandatory Functional Purpose
Every spoken exchange must satisfy at least one of these functions:
- **Advance Plot**: "I located the murder weapon, buried under the bridge embankment."
- **Expose Character**: "I don't trust the bureau. They've never protected people like us."
- **Spark Friction**: "You lied to me. Every single word was fabricated."
- **Deliver Urgent Lore**: "The detonation countdown triggers at three sharp."
- **Reveal Psychological Fracture**: "I... I don't know who I'm looking at anymore."
- **Plant Suspense**: "Do you honestly believe you were the only survivor?"

*Eliminate Phatic Filler*: Cut polite pleasantries, weather commentary, and generic conversational stallings.

### 2. Conciseness and Velocity
Spoken speech differs from essayistic prose. Prune decorative or redundant phrasing.
- *Wordy*: "What I am trying to tell you is that it is my sincere opinion that we should vacate the premises immediately." $\to$ *Lean*: "We leave right now."
- *Wordy*: "I am terribly sorry, but I do not possess the answer you are requesting." $\to$ *Lean*: "I don't know."

### 3. Natural Speech Is Imperfect
Authentic dialogue thrives on:
- Interruptions and overlaps.
- Hesitations and defensive deflection.
- Changing subjects abruptly.
- Trailing off mid-thought.
- Suggestion and subtext rather than blunt literalism.

### 4. Differentiated Character Voices
- A street informant sounds distinct from a seasoned magistrate.
- Leverage varied sentence structures, lexical choices, and rhythmic cadences so speakers are recognizable without dialogue tags.

### 5. Dialogue Tag Restraint
- Eliminate melodrama adverbs ("he snarled angrily").
- Let physical action and kinetic beats ground the tone ("He dropped the dossier onto the mahogany desk. 'Talk.'").
