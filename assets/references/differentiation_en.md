# Creative Differentiation & Novelty Design

This reference prevents narratives from collapsing into predictable, high-frequency genre tropes.

## High-Level Genre Prompts Demand Immediate Differentiation

When the user specifies only a broad genre descriptor:
- Urban
- Fantasy
- Mystery / Procedural
- Romance
- Sci-Fi

This **does not license** defaulting to standard genre clichés. You must construct differentiated creative anchors before finalizing outlines.

## The Five Dimensions of Differentiation

### 1. Protagonist Dimension
- Is the origin archetype cliché (e.g., chosen one, betrayed prodigy, reincarnated avenger)?
- Are initial advantages/drawbacks overly predictable?
- What is the protagonist's profound internal motivation (autonomous agency versus passive reactivity)?
- What is their fatal psychological blind spot?

### 2. Conflict Dimension
- Is the core conflict merely the default trope of the genre?
- Does a secondary conflict subvert surface expectations?
- Does the central contradiction transform qualitatively in the middle arcs?

### 3. Worldbuilding Dimension
- Do cosmological and legal rules actively reshape character choices?
- Do societal, resource, and political structures continuously generate organic friction?
- Does the world maintain logical vitality independent of protagonist involvement?

### 4. Relational Dimension
- Are secondary figures constrained to static archetypes (pure ally, love interest, comic relief)?
- Do dynamic relationships feature mutual shaping, complicity, exploitation, and painful loyalty?
- Do interpersonal fractures actively redirect the macro plot?

### 5. Pacing & Narrative Engine Dimension
- Are payoffs redundant or repetitive?
- Are distinct reading motivators planned across successive phases?
- Does initial intrigue evolve naturally into deeper philosophical or existential stakes?
