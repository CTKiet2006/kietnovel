# Supplementary Style Reference for Suspense & Mystery

## Clue Placement

- Plant at least one clue (authentic clue or red herring) per chapter.
- Critical clues must surface at least twice before final revelation.
- The first appearance of a clue must blend seamlessly into the scene without artificial emphasis.

## Misdirection Techniques

- Red herrings must possess self-contained plausibility, never feeling entirely pointless in retrospect.
- Unreliable or withholding narrative framing must strictly respect the principle: "Never state an outright lie, but omit decisive framing."
- Shifting suspicion among suspects must stem from legitimate motives rather than arbitrary distraction.

## Pacing Control

- Implement undulating pacing: Tension $\to$ Brief Relief $\to$ Escalating Tension.
- Ration information releases: offer just enough to intrigue, but calibrated to leave the picture incomplete.
- End each chapter on an active suspense hook; never conclude passively.

## Logical Rigor

- Timelines must remain chronologically verifiable upon retrospective inspection.
- The triad of alibi, motive, and opportunity/method must be fully accounted for.
- When the truth unfolds, astute readers should be able to deduce the conclusion from previously established evidence.
