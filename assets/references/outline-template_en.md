# Outline Planning Template & Scale Calibration

This template guides Architects in assessing narrative scale before selecting structural outline granularity.

## Step 1: Calibrate Story Scale

### Short-Form / Single-Volume Narrative
- **Scope**: Single dominant conflict, unified goal, tight cast, definitive concentrated climax.
- **Reference Length**: 8-25 chapters.
- **Format**: Flat `outline`.

### Mid-Form / Multi-Stage Narrative
- **Scope**: Staged escalation, several subplots, evolving interpersonal dynamics.
- **Reference Length**: 25-60 chapters.
- **Format**: Flat `outline` or lightweight layered outline.

### Long-Form Serialized Narrative
- **Scope**: Continuous upgradability, sustained relational tension, multi-stage progression, expanding cosmos, overarching conspiracies or generational arcs.
- **Reference Length**: 80-200+ chapters.
- **Format**: Layered `layered_outline`.

## Step 2: Layered Outline Triggers

Prefer `layered_outline` whenever any 2 of the following apply:
- Worldbuilding unfolds progressively across multiple geographic or societal tiers.
- Protagonist power or status progression spans multiple qualitative thresholds.
- Core relationship dynamics evolve fundamentally across distinct eras.
- Distinct thematic conflicts govern early, middle, and late phases.
- The narrative demands shifts in jurisdiction, faction, identity, or grand objectives.

## Step 3: Rolling Expansion Architecture for Long-Form

Never draft a flat ledger of 100+ chapters upfront:
1. **Volume Skeleton**: Each volume resolves a macro conflict, featuring a volume climax and overarching cliffhanger.
2. **Arc Decomposition**: Structure 8-15 chapter arcs with explicit goals and emotional payoffs.
3. **Rolling Expansion**: Detail chapters strictly for Arc 1; keep subsequent arcs and volumes as skeletons (`title`, `goal`, `estimated_chapters`), expanding them dynamically via `expand_next_arc` as writing proceeds.
