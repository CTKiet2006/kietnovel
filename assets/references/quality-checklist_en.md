# Chapter Quality Checklist

Use this checklist prior to chapter delivery and commitment to ensure structural and prose excellence.

## Holistic Audit

### Foundational Elements
- [ ] **Definitive Chapter Title**: Relevant, evocative, avoids ending spoilers.
- [ ] **Target Word Count**: Meets pacing and length expectations.
- [ ] **Chapter Integrity**: Complete narrative arc with inciting disturbance, escalation, climax, and unresolved question.
- [ ] **Spatial & Temporal Grounding**: Readers clearly track where and when scenes take place.

## Chapter Opening
- [ ] **Engage Within 3 Paragraphs**: Open with kinetic action, conflict, or immediate intrigue—never mundane weather reports.
- [ ] **Connect with Preceding Chapter**: Address previous fallout or establish clear temporal shifts.
- [ ] **Zero Info-Dumping**: Weave context organically into active engagement.

## Narrative & Plot Advancement
- [ ] **Indispensable Core Event**: The chapter delivers a development that cannot be deleted without breaking the plot.
- [ ] **Drives Main Plot**: Unveils fresh information, shifts relationships, or escalates stakes.
- [ ] **Internal Logic & Plausibility**: Strict causal chains; no reliance on contrived coincidences.
- [ ] **Dynamic Tension**: Undulating pacing alternating between high pressure and atmospheric breathing room.

## Character & Dialogue
- [ ] **Behavioral Consistency**: Actions align with established agency and psychology.
- [ ] **Distinct Character Voices**: Differentiated sentence lengths, registers, and linguistic habits.
- [ ] **Purposive Dialogue**: Every exchange reveals leverage, escalates friction, or drops critical lore.
- [ ] **Rich Subtext**: Spoken lines conceal hidden motives and tactical intent.

## Show, Don't Tell Standards
- [ ] Eliminate raw emotion labeling (replace "he was angry" with somatic cues: clenched knuckles, rigid breathing).
- [ ] Evoke concrete sensory textures (tactile resistance, acoustic shifts, pungent scents) over abstract adjectives.

## Suspense & Hooks
- [ ] **Active End-of-Chapter Hook**: Conclude on an unresolved danger, choice, or revelation.
- [ ] **No False Tension**: Never deflate dramatic tension with trivial misunderstandings.

## Prose & Anti-AI Polish
- [ ] **Zero AI Vocabulary**: Eradicate cliché transition fillers ("furthermore", "delve", "testament to", "palpable").
- [ ] **Rhythmic Variation**: Alternate terse, punchy lines with flowing sentences.
