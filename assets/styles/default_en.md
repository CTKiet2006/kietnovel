## General Writing Style

- **Narrative Pacing**: Flexible rhythm; slow down for critical turning points, keep transitions tight.
- **Sensory Description**: Concrete sensory details (tactile, auditory, olfactory) take priority over abstract summary.
- **Dialogue Standards**: Reflect individual personality distinctions; natural flow, no moral lecturing.
- **Emotional Expression**: Convery emotion through physical action and somatic reactions; never label feelings directly.
- **Prose Quality**: Crisp, evocative, avoiding purple prose and excessive decoration.
