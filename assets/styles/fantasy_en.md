## Fantasy & Adventure Style

- **Worldbuilding Exposure**: Avoid dense exposition dumps; reveal cosmological and societal rules organically through character action and environmental interaction.
- **Magic & Power Systems**: Enforce explicit costs, limits, and drawbacks; forbid omnipotent abilities; showcase system boundaries during intense conflicts.
- **Epic Scope**: Weave sweeping historical stakes with intimate personal arcs; allow humble protagonist perspectives to illuminate vast geopolitical tides.
- **Cultures & Factions**: Distinct factions and races must exhibit differentiated linguistic habits, moral codes, and cultural instincts.
- **Combat Choreography**: Emphasize tactical positioning, tactical sacrifice, and situational exploitation over raw power scaling.
- **Journey Dynamics**: Each milestone setting must present distinct sensorial and geographic texture—never repeat generic village or tavern archetypes.
- **Progression Arc**: Magical or martial growth must remain inextricably linked to psychological maturation; power is earned through irreversible sacrifice.
- **Nomenclature & Register**: Maintain tonal consistency in terminology, avoiding jarring modern idioms that shatter immersive fantasy realism.
