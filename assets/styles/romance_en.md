## Romance & Emotional Drama Style

- **Emotional Progression**: Follow organic development: Contact $\to$ Rapport $\to$ Friction $\to$ Reconciliation $\to$ Deep intimacy; do not rush romantic milestones.
- **Relational Tension**: Each stage demands plausible friction stemming from authentic roots (temperament, conflicting obligations, misunderstanding, external pressure).
- **Interior Depth**: Explore character vulnerability and internal conflict without lapsing into prolonged, clinical psychoanalysis.
- **Subtle Interactions**: Convey affection via micro-expressions, subtle habits, and inadvertent physical cues rather than blunt exposition.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Elliptical and charged during early courtship; effortless during intimacy; pointed yet restrained during conflict.
- **Atmospheric Resonance**: Echo emotional landscapes through evocative settings without leaning on clichés (e.g., downpours signaling heartbreak).
- **Secondary Characters**: Friends and foils must possess autonomous narrative momentum, rather than functioning merely as relationship sounding boards.
- **Conflict Foundation**: Resist misunderstandings resolvable by a single conversation; root disputes in fundamental differences of core values or worldview.
