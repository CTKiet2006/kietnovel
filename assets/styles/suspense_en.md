## Suspense & Mystery Style

- **Narrative Structure**: Interwoven multi-threaded narrative; leverage information gaps to sustain suspense; reveal truth in calibrated layers.
- **Misdirection (Red Herrings)**: Plant credible false leads; exploit perspective blind spots to misdirect reader deductions organically.
- **Clue Management**: Vital clues must surface at least twice before final revelation, without drawing premature attention.
- **Pacing Dynamics**: Alternate tension and release; end chapters on compelling suspense hooks; decelerate before climaxes to accumulate narrative pressure.
- **Atmospheric Immersion**: Environmental descriptions amplify unease; harness lighting, acoustic shifts, and weather dynamics to build dread.
- **Character Motivation**: Every action must stem from coherent character agency; avoid contrived choices made solely to move the plot forward.
- **Subtextual Dialogue**: Layer spoken lines with implicit subtext; structure confrontations and interrogations like tactical duels.
- **Truth Revelation**: Never rely on unearned coincidence or surprise evidence; revelations must reward re-reading through visible foreshadowing.
